02 — questions

Before you ask

Why is elethiya.online shutting down?

It's no longer in active use, and keeping an idle domain registered and pointed at live infrastructure isn't worth the upkeep. It will be permanently closed after a short wind-down period.

Do I need to change anything?

If you have links, bookmarks, or integrations pointing at the .online domain, update them directly to elethiya.com. If you are looking for our portfolio, please visit elethiya.in.

Will content move automatically?

Core content is mirrored across the new domains already. Anything you've linked directly should still be re-pointed manually, since the old domain won't resolve once it's decommissioned.

What is elethiya.in used for now?

elethiya.in has been transitioned into ELETHIYA's official portfolio website. It now acts as the central hub for showcasing all of our creative work and projects.
